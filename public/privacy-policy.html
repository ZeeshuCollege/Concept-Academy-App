Privacy Policy

Concept Academy Mobile Application

Last Updated: September 28, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Overview & Institute Mandate

Concept Academy ("we", "our", or "the Institute") operates the Concept Academy mobile application exclusively as an academic management companion for enrolled students, verified parents, faculty members, and institute administrators.

This application is designed specifically for offline coaching institute operations (Standards 9–10 Foundation, Standards 11–12 Science, JEE Main/Advanced, and NEET coaching). We do not sell data to advertisers, and we collect only the information strictly necessary to provide academic tracking, attendance, test grading, timetable scheduling, and fee record administration.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The application requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary for educational functionality:

  • INTERNET: Required for secure API communication with the Supabase backend database, downloading syllabus PDFs, and syncing marks.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Required to alert students and parents about upcoming lectures, schedule changes, test results, attendance statuses, and fee account updates.
  • CAMERA (Optional): Used solely if the user chooses to capture a new profile photo or scan a physical worksheet. It is never accessed in the background.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional): Used solely if the user chooses to select an existing photo from their gallery for their profile picture.

No location permissions, microphone permissions, contact permissions, or background tracking permissions are requested or used by this application.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted enterprise-grade infrastructure providers operating under strict confidentiality:

  • Supabase Inc. — Database hosting, user authentication, and encrypted data storage. Hosted on AWS infrastructure with SOC 2 compliance.
  • Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp Business) — Used exclusively to dispatch one-time passwords (OTPs) to verified parent and student phone numbers for password recovery.

5. Children & Minors Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

Concept Academy provides secondary and higher secondary coaching. Our enrolled students include teenagers aged 13 to 18, as well as adult learners and parents.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verified parental consent.
  • Parent accounts are explicitly linked to their child's account so that parents can supervise all academic notices, attendance records, test marks, and fee payment details.
  • No behavioral profiling, ad tracking, or public social networking features exist in the application.

6. Data Security & Storage

All communications between the mobile application and our backend database are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database rows are protected with PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring that users can only access information relevant to their assigned role and profile.

7. Account Deletion & Data Retention Rights

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and privacy regulations, users have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

In-App Deletion Path:

Open the Concept Academy App → Profile Tab → Security & Privacy → Delete Account & Data.

Web-Based Deletion Request:

You can also request immediate account and data deletion online through our dedicated portal:
Submit Account Deletion Request →

Upon receiving an account deletion request, we permanently delete the user's login credentials, profile data, personal photos, and direct links within 30 days, retaining only legally required physical financial receipts as mandated by applicable accounting regulations.
